FTC Scoreboard

About

This will allow you to display a scoreboard on a TV as if you are at an official FTC event and control it directly from your phone. Perfect for team scrimmages, practice fields, and driver tryouts.

Simply create a room, open the Scoreboard Display on a large monitor, TV, or projector, and use the Admin Control link on your mobile device to manage the match in real time.
